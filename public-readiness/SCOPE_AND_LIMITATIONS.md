# Public Scope and Limitations

## Purpose

This document defines the public interpretation boundary for Shadow-Tech2030's internal compliance-readiness results.

## Public scope

The published result covers only the **internal compliance-readiness process and its aggregate outcome**.

Publicly disclosed information is intentionally limited to:

- aggregate control status;
- aggregate gap status;
- aggregate finding status;
- aggregate evidence count;
- automated readiness-check results;
- repository-test results;
- explicit limitations and assurance boundaries.

## Excluded from public disclosure

The public package excludes detailed internal material, including:

- proprietary source code;
- product or infrastructure architecture;
- security-sensitive implementation details;
- internal control procedures at operational depth;
- internal risks and threat scenarios;
- vulnerability specifics;
- remediation details;
- supplier-specific assessments;
- identity and access details;
- personnel information;
- private repository content;
- cryptographic keys, credentials or secrets.

## Assurance limitation

`INTERNAL_READINESS_COMPLETE` and `READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_ASSESSMENT` are **internal readiness states**.

They are not external certifications, attestations or audit opinions.

## Timing limitation

The results reflect the state of the internal readiness program at the time the final review was executed. Future material changes may require reassessment.

## Evidence limitation

The public package does not expose the complete internal evidence set. The underlying internal records are maintained separately and may be presented selectively to authorized external assessors under appropriate confidentiality controls.

## No source-code disclosure assumption

An external assurance engagement does not automatically require transfer of proprietary source code. Shadow-Tech2030's preferred approach is to use sufficient alternative evidence where appropriate, while allowing the external assessor to determine whether that evidence satisfies the applicable assurance objective.
